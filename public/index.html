Room types & rate plans

Each room type is sold through one or more rate plans, e.g. room only (EP), with breakfast (CP), half board (MAP). A derived plan takes its price from another plan plus or minus an amount or percentage.

Occupancy-based pricing

Raise (or lower) prices automatically as rooms fill up. When several rules apply, the one with the highest occupancy threshold wins.
